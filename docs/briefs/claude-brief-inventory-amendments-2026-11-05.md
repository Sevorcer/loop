# LOOP Inventory — Mandatory Amendments to the Design Brief
Status: Collin confirmed 2026-11-05 — these are REQUIRED fixes to Field_service_inventory_design.md, not optional suggestions. No inventory lane is briefed to Claude without them.
Source review: ~/workspace/your_files/loop-inventory-design-review-2026-11-05.md

## A. De-duplication (fix in schema, Lane 1)
1. **One item master.** Service Item List (#36, Allison's seed) and Inventory Catalog are ONE catalog: sellable facet (price/member price) + stockable facet (SKU, cost ref, on-hand). No separate service_items vs inventory_items catalogs that can drift. Items may be sell-only, stock-only, or both.
2. **Serials: one truth.** `inventory_serial_units` is authoritative; `installed_systems.serial_numbers` links/syncs to it, never an independent copy.
3. **Material plans generate from estimates.** Where a job has `estimate_line_items`/`job_line_items`, plan lines are generated by matching them to SKUs. Unmatched lines surface as "needs SKU" — plans are not re-typed by hand.
4. **Vehicles: migrate, don't add a third concept.** New `vehicles` table must migrate `crews.truck_name` and `vehicle_alerts.vehicle_name` onto the FK and retire those free-text fields.
5. **Purchasing stays in its lane.** Lane 5 = PO/receiving/stock only. No AP, invoicing, or bid features — QuickBooks and Joist already own those.

## B. Behavior fixes (Lanes 2–4)
6. **Readiness includes on-order + ETA.** States per line: OK / short / arriving-in-time (on-order ETA ≤ job date) / at-risk (ETA > job date or not ordered). On-hand-only checking is not acceptable.
7. **Reservations expire/release.** When a job's date moves or it cancels, its reservations release and readiness re-runs automatically. No indefinite stock locks.
8. **Staged enforcement, nothing hard-blocks at launch.** Readiness: warn-only first. Closeout reconciliation: warn → office-override soft block → hard block, only after templates/counts prove accurate. Applies to both gates.
9. **Small seed catalog.** Serialized equipment + top ~50 consumables actually pulled. Do NOT bulk-seed the 416-item Service Fusion product catalog.
10. **Lane priority split.** Install = kit/pallet flow first (Lane 4 is the value). Service-van replenishment (Lane 2 van features) must not block or delay Lane 4.
11. **Nameplate scan in Lane 3.** Serial capture uses the already-specced nameplate scan (feature A); manual typing is the fallback, not the primary flow.
12. **Refrigerant by weight.** Serialized cylinders PLUS lbs used/recovered logged per job line (EPA log). R-454B and R-410A remain separate SKUs.
13. **RMA/warranty returns in Lane 3, not Lane 5.** Quarantine location + return-to-supplier flow ships with serialized units — warranty parts returns are routine, not a purchasing edge case.
